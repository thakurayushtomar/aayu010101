# Demo Script — Team Optimus (about 2–3 minutes)

**Opening (15 seconds)**  
"Hello, we are Team Optimus. Our project is Earth Jukebox. Earth science is often presented visually, which can exclude people who are blind or have low vision. We turn Earth visual information into sound while keeping the visual and text explanations available."

**Challenge core (60 seconds)**  
"Here is our EIC Jukebox mode. This is NASA Scientific Visualization Studio item 5313, Change in Night Lights between 2012 and 2023 — EIC Version. As the video plays, our browser samples frames, measures their average luminance and maps that value to a note in a musical scale. The sound is generated by our own code in real time; it is not background music or AI-generated music."

Play the EIC video and generated sound. Explain: "Higher sampled brightness creates a higher note. This is a simple visual-pattern sonification, not a calibrated measurement of radiance."

**Accessibility (30 seconds)**  
"Users can navigate with a keyboard, hear the mapping explanation, and use the screen-reader-friendly controls. Voice search is optional; text and coordinate entry are alternatives. The chart has a data-table alternative."

**Additional feature (30 seconds)**  
"Our global explorer adds a way to select any coordinate and explore six NASA POWER environmental parameters over a selected year. This is supporting data exploration; we keep it separate from the required EIC visualization track."

**Honest limitations and close (20 seconds)**  
"We disclose that NASA POWER is gridded data and that video pixel sampling can depend on browser cross-origin permissions. We document the mapping, source credits and limitations. Earth Jukebox aims to make Earth science more accessible by letting people see, hear and read the same story."
