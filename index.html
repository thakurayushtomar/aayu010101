01 / VERIFIED EIC VISUAL

Change in Night Lights · 2012–2023

NASA SVS ID 5313

An EIC-version visualization showing annual average night lights and change across the globe. NASA SVS identifies this item as EIC Version.

How this EIC track becomes sound

At regular intervals, the application samples the displayed video frame, measures its average luminance and maps that measured brightness to a note in a pentatonic scale. Brighter sampled frames produce higher notes. The sound is synthesized in the browser while the video plays; it is not the video's soundtrack.

This first EIC track uses whole-frame average brightness as a simple visual feature. It does not claim that brightness alone measures night-light intensity scientifically; the video includes titles, transitions and map areas. Listen as a visual-pattern sonification, not as a calibrated radiance measurement.

Press Play EIC with sound. If browser cross-origin restrictions prevent frame analysis, download the NASA video and load it using the local file option.
